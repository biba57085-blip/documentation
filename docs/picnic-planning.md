# Picnic Planning Checklist

A good picnic is easy to set up, easy to clean up, and comfortable enough that nobody wants to leave early.

## Food Ideas

- Sandwiches or wraps.
- Fresh fruit.
- Cut vegetables with dip.
- Crackers, cheese, and olives.
- Cookies or brownies.

## Packing List

- Blanket or folding chairs.
- Reusable plates, cups, and utensils.
- Napkins and trash bags.
- Cooler with ice packs.
- Sunscreen and bug spray.
- Water for everyone.

## Location Tips

Choose a spot with shade, nearby restrooms, and enough space for the group. Check park rules ahead of time if you plan to bring a grill, pets, or amplified music.
