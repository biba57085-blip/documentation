# Houseplant Care Basics

Houseplants usually do best with consistent light, careful watering, and occasional pruning. Most problems come from too much water rather than too little.

## Light

Place plants near a window that matches their needs:

- Bright direct light for succulents and cacti.
- Bright indirect light for pothos, monstera, and many tropical plants.
- Lower light for snake plants and ZZ plants.

## Watering

Check soil moisture before watering. If the top inch of soil is dry, many common plants are ready for water. Empty drainage trays so roots do not sit in standing water.

## Maintenance

- Rotate pots every few weeks for even growth.
- Wipe dusty leaves with a damp cloth.
- Remove yellow or dead leaves.
- Repot when roots circle the container or grow through drainage holes.
