# Backyard Stargazing Notes

Backyard stargazing is a simple way to learn the night sky without buying expensive equipment. A clear view of the horizon, a comfortable chair, and a little patience are enough to get started.

## What to Bring

- A warm layer, even in mild weather.
- A red flashlight to preserve night vision.
- A notebook for recording observations.
- Binoculars, if available.

## Easy Targets

1. **The Moon** — Look for craters, shadows, and changing phases.
2. **Orion** — A recognizable winter constellation with three bright belt stars.
3. **The Big Dipper** — A useful pattern for finding Polaris in the northern sky.
4. **Bright planets** — Venus, Jupiter, Mars, and Saturn are often visible without a telescope.

## Tips

Give your eyes 20 to 30 minutes to adjust to darkness. Avoid looking at phone screens unless they are dimmed and color-shifted red.
